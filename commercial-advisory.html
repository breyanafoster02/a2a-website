What we add

Cash flow velocity

How fast cash moves through your business, from invoice to collection and from payable to payment. We show you where it slows down and what that will do to your balance 30, 60, and 90 days from now.

Burn-rate trend analysis

Not just what you spent, but how your spending rate is changing, and how many months of runway that leaves you.

Variance risk flagging

Your budget compared to actuals every month, with the variances that matter flagged and explained, so you can fix them before they compound.
