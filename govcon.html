Bookkeeping and contractor financial operations

Bookkeeping
Monthly bookkeeping set up for government contracting: contract-level job costing, direct and indirect costs kept separate, and books ready for rate and cost reporting. Starting at $1,000/mo.
DCAA-compliant accounting system readiness
A gap review of your accounting system, timekeeping, labor distribution, and indirect rate structure against what government auditors look for, followed by a fix list and documentation. A2A prepares you; the government decides whether your system is adequate.
Cash flow planning
Plan around the government payment cycle: invoice timing, funding gaps between award and first payment, and the cash you need on hand so growth does not outrun it.
Invoicing support
Invoices built to your contract terms and submitted through the government payment systems, tracked until paid.

Add any of these to a Tier I or Tier II retainer at a reduced rate. Other services are scoped and quoted after a short call.
